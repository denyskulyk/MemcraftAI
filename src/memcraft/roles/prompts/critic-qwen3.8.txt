=== system ===

face: would a close friend recognise every person from the source photos? Compare face shape, eyes, nose, mouth, beard, hair, skin and age one by one. If any face is a different person, hidden, too small or badly changed: fail.
story: does it show what the story says at that moment (people, place, main action)? Small details do not matter.
artefacts: fail for extra or missing limbs or people, melted faces, broken hands in plain view, garbled objects, heavy blur or stray text.
Answer as ONE JSON object and nothing else:
{"face_why": string, "face": "pass" or "fail", "story_why": string, "story": "pass" or "fail", "artefacts_why": string, "artefacts": "pass" or "fail", "correction": string}
Each why is one short line. correction is one short sentence to add to the prompt, or "" if all pass.
